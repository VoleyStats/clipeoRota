Clipeo Rota is an app for volleyball coaches to prepare the rotation for each set and hand it to the referee with a QR code. This policy explains what data the app handles.

## Controller

[owner]. Contact: hola@clipeostats.com

## What data the app handles

Clipeo Rota **does not collect personal data** and does not send any data to a server. The app needs no account, does not use an internet connection, and includes no advertising, analytics or third-party services.

The app stores **only on your device**:

- the profile (coach or referee) and the format you chose last;
- the three-character team code;
- the player numbers for each zone and each set;
- in the referee profile, the last two rotations scanned.

This data never leaves your device, except when you show the QR code to someone so they can scan it. The QR code contains only the team code, the format, the set number and the player numbers; it does not contain names or any other player data.

## Camera

The app asks for camera permission only in the referee profile, to read QR codes. Camera images are processed on the device at that moment and are neither stored nor sent anywhere.

## Children

The app does not ask for or store names, dates of birth, photos or any other player data, so it can be used with youth teams.

## How to delete your data

You can delete the team code and the rotations with «Nuevo partido» (New match) in the coach view. Uninstalling the app deletes all the data it stores.

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version at this same address with its update date.
